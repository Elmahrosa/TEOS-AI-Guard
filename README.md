## 🌍 Vision

**TEOS-AI-Guard** is the compliance-first, AI-powered security and governance layer for all TEOS dApps and Elmahrosa products.

It enforces **audit-ready identity, modular compliance, and SDG-aligned guardrails**, ensuring trusted and scalable civic innovation for governments, banks, and developers.

---

## 👥 Who This Repo Is For

- Governments & ministries exploring compliant AI + blockchain  
- Banks & fintechs requiring audit-ready AI systems  
- Developers building civic or SDG-aligned dApps  
- Institutions seeking Pi-native identity infrastructure  

---

## 🛡 Compliance Guardrails

- **DSPM (Data Security Posture Management):** Discover and classify data before AI sees it.  
- **DLP (Data Loss Prevention):** Enforce one policy across cloud, web, email, endpoints, APIs.  
- **DDR (Data Detection & Response):** Continuous monitoring, incident readiness.  
- **Audit Logs:** Tamper-proof, privacy-preserving, regulator-ready.  
- **Jurisdiction Awareness:** AI behavior adapts to sector and country regulations.  

---

## 🏛 Civic-First Capabilities

- **Identity & Governance Layer** → Pi-based KYC/AML, proposal submission, transparent voting.  
- **Financial Infrastructure** → $TEOS utility token, $ERT stablecoin, Nile DEX.  
- **Civic Services** → Smart City dashboard, land registry, SDG progress monitoring.  
- **Cultural Economy** → TEOS NFT AI Generator, heritage tokenization, TutTEOS education.  
- **AI Gateway** → Sanctioned endpoints, explainability, role-based permissions.
